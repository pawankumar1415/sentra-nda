## 2. Narrative Guidance Standards (The "10-Point Check")
The AI is instructed to validate narratives against these specific criteria:
1.  **Clear Delivery Position**: Must include a DCA (Delivery Confidence Assessment) with a "because" statement.
2.  **Benefits/Milestones**: Clear status on project benefits.
3.  **Cost and Schedule**: Explicit position on financial and timeline metrics.
4.  **Baseline RAG**: Reason for the current Red/Amber/Green status.
5.  **Capability & Capacity**: RAG status for resources.
6.  **Audience Appropriateness**: Tone suitable for leadership.
7.  **Acronyms**: Expanded on first use (e.g., "Civil Nuclear Constabulary (CNC)").
8.  **Dates**: Written in full (e.g., 26 February 2026).
9.  **Formatting**: Flowing paragraphs instead of bullet points.
10. **Alignment**: Ensuring the RAG status matches the written narrative.
